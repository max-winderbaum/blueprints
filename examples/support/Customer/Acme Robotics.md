# Acme Robotics
## Who they are
Warehouse automation company in Columbus, Ohio. About 400 staff. They fit our high-bay LED arrays across three fulfilment centres.
## What they buy from us
Series 900 high-bay arrays with the networked driver. Roughly 1,200 units on the current order form, delivered in two waves.
## Watch out for
Their facilities lead, Tom Reyes, expects a same-day reply on anything that stops an install crew. Their order form has a replacement-parts clause with a five business day ceiling, so shipping delays become contract issues fast.
