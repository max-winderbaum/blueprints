# Ticket 4411
## What happened
Acme's welcome kit for Columbus East arrived without the setup card for the networked driver. Tom Reyes reported it at 07:40 on 19 August. The install crew could not pair the first array without it.
## What we did
Emailed a PDF of the setup card within the hour so the crew could keep working. Shipped a printed replacement the same day. Because a missing card counts as a missing part under [[Replacement parts]], the five business day clock started on 19 August. The card arrived 20 August.
