# Replacement parts
## Plain English
If anything in a delivered kit is missing or dead on arrival, we ship a replacement within five business days of being told. Documentation that the crew needs to install counts as a part.
## Exact wording
"Supplier shall dispatch a replacement for any Component reported missing or non-functional on delivery within five (5) Business Days of written notice from Customer. For the purposes of this Section 9.2, Component includes any documentation required to commission the Products."
