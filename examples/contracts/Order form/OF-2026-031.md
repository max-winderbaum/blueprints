# OF-2026-031
## Summary
First order form under the Acme MSA. Supply and commissioning of 1,200 Series 900 high-bay arrays across Columbus East, Columbus West, and Dayton.
## Key dates
Signed 20 June 2026. Wave one commissioning due 31 August 2026. Wave two due 30 September 2026. A wave more than 30 days late lets Acme withhold 10 percent of that wave's price until it is commissioned.
## What was ordered
600 units for wave one, 600 for wave two, each with the networked driver and a five year warranty.
