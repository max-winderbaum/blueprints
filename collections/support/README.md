# Support

Everything the Lumen Fixtures support team tracks: who our customers are, how their onboarding is going, and every ticket they raise.

Owner: Dana Okafor, Head of Support.

Tickets may point at the agreement they fall under, which lives in the Contracts Collection. We never copy contract terms into a ticket. We link to them.

## Kinds of Thing

* Person, keyed by handle.
* Customer, keyed by CRM id.
* Program, a dated push that groups onboardings.
* Onboarding, one customer's path to their first live site.
* Ticket, one reported problem.
* Escalation, a Ticket that needs a decision above its owner.
